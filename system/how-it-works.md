# How it works, and why

## Three folders that are yours, one that is ours

`system/` is ours: the procedures. `me/`, `sessions/`, and `loops/` are yours: the life. Updates replace `system/` whole and never touch the rest, so the system can improve weekly while your house stays exactly as you left it. **Why:** the alternative is updates that merge into your identity files, which sooner or later overwrite something you meant.

## Precedence

The agent reads `system/`, then `me/`, then the recent record. Where they speak to the same thing, **`me/` wins**: your preferences outrank our defaults. One exception: the floor in `system/charlie-echo.md` (honesty, mirror-before-agree, confirm-before-acting) is not adjustable. **Why:** tuning should shape the voice, not license an agent that flatters or acts unasked.

## Memory you own

Nothing about you lives where you can't read it. Every close writes a note; every open reads the last one back in a line. **Why the read-back:** you are the only reliable drift detector; hearing your own record daily is what catches an agent (or a file) going quietly wrong. The weekly retrospective asks once: "anything in how I've been answering that's off?" Vendor hidden memory is turned off at setup and checked at every close.

## The record compounds

Sessions (one note each, `YYYY-MM-DD-Snnn-<key-words>.md`) roll into weekly summaries, weeklies into monthlies, monthlies into quarterlies. **Why:** the open stays fast (read the summary plus the week, not the archive), and when someone asks for your readout, it is already written.

## Loops

A loop is an open thread of work: bigger than a task, smaller than a project. One file each in `loops/`, with a status line. The brief reads them; the close updates them. Projects live wherever your projects live; loops are the harness's own working memory of what's unfinished.

## The depth words

**elaborate** (more detail), **explain** (the why), **analyze** (the structured pass: alternatives, consequences, recommendation). Same shape every time, so a short default costs you nothing.

## Connectors

Day one needs none: you type and paste, the harness reads and writes files. Mail, calendar, and chat connectors are an upgrade, not a prerequisite, and they belong to a conversation with your IT lead: see [it-note.md](it-note.md).
